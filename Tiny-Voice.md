# Project: Tiny-Voice (稚语)
## Positioning: Developmental Translator for "Horrible 3" Toddlers

### Core Vision
Bridge the cognitive gap between a toddler's emotional explosion and a parent's understanding. Transform tantrums from "stress events" into "developmental milestones" using acoustic intelligence and child psychology.

### Key Features
#### 1. The Tantrum Translator (Brut $\rightarrow$ Pro)
- **Input:** High-decibel screams, repetitive "No!", fragmented phrases.
- **Analysis:** Local LLM maps emotional spikes to developmental needs (e.g., autonomy, sensory overload, frustration).
- **Output:** "Your child isn't hating the shoes; they are exercising their need for **Autonomy**. Suggestion: Provide limited choices."

#### 2. Preemptive Alert System
- **Acoustic Monitoring:** ESP32-S3 monitors speech rate and pitch.
- **Warning:** Detects "pre-explosion" patterns $\rightarrow$ Triggers a notification to parents' devices.
- **Parent-Zen Integration:** Automatically triggers calming music or ambient sounds in parents' headphones to maintain a "gentle" state before intervening.

#### 3. Growth Mirror (Long-term Asset)
- **Pattern Tracking:** Log the frequency and triggers of tantrums.
- **Milestone Mapping:** Track the transition from "Screaming" $\rightarrow$ "Crying" $\rightarrow$ "Verbalizing Need."
- **Outcome:** A quantified map of the child's emotional regulation growth.

### Technical Stack
- **Edge:** ESP32-S3 $\rightarrow$ Local Server.
- **Intelligence:** Local LLM prompted with Piaget/Montessori developmental frameworks.
- **Actuation:** Integration with Home Assistant for lighting/audio cues.

### Success Metric
Reduction in parental stress levels and increase in "gentle parenting" consistency.
